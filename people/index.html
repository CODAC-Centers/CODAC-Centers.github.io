People

People

Each investigator brings a distinct combination of expertise, and the team was assembled to cover the domains the plan requires. Every task has a responsible lead, but all investigators collaborate across the thrusts. Graduate students, research scientists and postdocs across the partner labs do the day-to-day work of the thrusts.

The team includes a non-academic partner, the Topos Institute, international leaders in developing and applying category theory to real-world problems. Sharing mathematical results and software implementations across thrusts is critical to the success of each one.
