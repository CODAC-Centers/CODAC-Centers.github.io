Compositional Optimization Algorithms

Leads: Hale, Pajic, and Dixon

Decentralized systems deployed in realistic environments have intermittent and unpredictable communications between agents, which creates challenges for the design and execution of decentralized algorithms.

This thrust uses cellular sheaves and homological programs to represent distributed problems, in order to systematically design algorithms that mitigate the impacts of impaired communications. Agents also often face nonconvex and mixed-integer problems, so the thrust develops new techniques that let agents jointly solve such problems even under asynchrony.
