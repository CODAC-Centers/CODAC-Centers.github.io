AFOSR awards the DIGICAMS Center of Excellence

The Air Force Office of Scientific Research has awarded the center for Designing Integrated, Generic, and Interoperable Compositional Analyses of Modern Systems (DIGICAMS), led by James Fairbanks, with co-investigators Matthew Hale, Warren Dixon, Miroslav Pajic, and Kristopher Brown, and with the Topos Institute as a non-academic partner.

The center develops unified mathematical frameworks for the composition of decision-making processes across optimization, dynamical systems, and control, organized into five research thrusts.
