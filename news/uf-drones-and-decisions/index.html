UF features the center's work on coordinating autonomous units

The University of Florida’s Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering has written up the center’s work under the headline Drones and decisions: UF optimizing communication among autonomous units.

The piece covers the problem the center exists to solve: a drone swarm or a fleet of self-driving vehicles has to handle optimization, dynamics and control at once, and the standard practice of treating those three separately is what lets a collection of individually sound subsystems add up to an unstable or wasteful whole. As James Fairbanks puts it there, the work “will develop compositional methods for decision-making in optimization, dynamical systems and control.”

The article notes the $3.5 million Air Force Research Laboratory University Center of Excellence award behind DIGICAMS, the investigators at UF, Georgia Tech and Duke, the partnership with the Topos Institute, and UF’s Autonomy Park as a place to fly the results.
